From Computer Applications to Data Analytics

Hello! I am Fathima Fida, a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) graduate from Calicut University. During my degree, my coursework in database management, statistics, and programming ignited my passion for understanding what lies beneath raw rows of data.

I am captivated by the entire data journey: from extracting and cleaning noisy real-world data with Python (Pandas & NumPy) and SQL, to engineering calculated metrics and bringing stories alive through Power BI and Excel dashboards.

Rather than relying on assumptions, I believe in empirical truth—finding correlation patterns, spotting outliers, and translating complex mathematical patterns into intuitive visual insights that help teams make informed decisions.
